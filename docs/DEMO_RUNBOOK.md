# EcoSphere AI Demonstration Runbook

## Demonstration objective

Demonstrate a coherent sustainability operating loop instead of isolated screens. Every outcome shown in the flow is either a seeded demonstration value or the result of a deterministic calculation.

| Step | Presenter action | Expected observation | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Open AIEM Campus Pilot. | The command center identifies the campus, marks simulated data, and shows EcoScore plus four resource indicators. | Establishes a clear operating baseline. |
| 2 | Point to the energy pulse. | The chart shows seeded hourly readings, a forecast snapshot, and confidence label. | Shows monitoring and prediction. |
| 3 | Select **Run live demo**. | A distinct energy spike appears in the trend and a high-severity HVAC alert opens. | Shows controlled anomaly detection. |
| 4 | Open the alert center. | Severity, measured value, threshold, status, and a practical next action are visible. | Shows operational response, not merely analytics. |
| 5 | Ask the advisor about the alert. | The advisor uses the displayed simulated telemetry and recommends a concise next step. | Shows AI as an explanation layer, not a data source. |
| 6 | Open What-if Sustainability Studio. | Enter or retain a 15% energy reduction. | Moves from diagnosis to decision support. |
| 7 | Run the simulation. | Avoided energy, CO₂e, electricity cost, EcoScore lift, and rank update. | Shows measurable intervention evaluation. |
| 8 | Show SDG cards. | SDGs 7, 11, 12, and 13 receive bounded impact indicators. | Connects operations to sustainability framing. |
| 9 | Show monitoring cadence. | The interface explains owner notifications and the prepared scheduled follow-up design. | Demonstrates continuity beyond an open browser tab. |
| 10 | Show data-ingestion panel. | Approved CSV import is available while sensor/API sources remain controlled. | Shows a credible path beyond seeded data. |

## Recommended presenter narration

> “EcoSphere AI starts with the operational signal. The simulation is clearly marked, but the same pipeline is ready for approved campus data. When the HVAC spike is injected, the backend compares it against the previous operating baseline, opens an alert, and can notify the owner. Instead of stopping at a chart, we use the simulator to test interventions and see carbon and cost outcomes before acting.”

## Reset guidance

The controlled injection intentionally avoids duplicate unresolved HVAC alerts. Resolve the active alert after a rehearsal, then refresh the page before the next demonstration. The seeded baseline is retained for reproducible comparisons.
