# EcoSphere AI — Website Design Directions

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Field Operations Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A confident, editorial operations interface that treats sustainability as a disciplined daily practice. Warm paper, deep moss, technical redaction marks, and measured data visualizations create a credible pilot identity rather than a generic “green tech” look.
**Probability:** 0.06

### Theme Name: Coastal Climate Atlas
**Very Brief Intro:** A bright cartographic direction rooted in Goa’s coastal ecology, using contour lines, field notes, and ocean-to-forest color transitions. It would feel civic, optimistic, and grounded in place.
**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Clear Signal Terminal
**Very Brief Intro:** A spare, high-contrast control-room system with monochrome surfaces, precise status lamps, and typographic hierarchy. It would emphasize vigilance, traceability, and operational trust.
**Probability:** 0.08

## Chosen direction: Field Operations Ledger

### Design Movement
**Contemporary editorial systems design** with references to field journals, transit operations boards, and research-lab publications. The website presents EcoSphere as a practical decision instrument, not an abstract AI promise.

### Core Principles
1. **Evidence before flourish:** Every visual hierarchy reinforces the distinction between simulated data, deterministic calculations, and AI explanation.
2. **Operational calm:** Dense information is organized through large type, generous margins, structured dividers, and quiet surfaces rather than dashboard clutter.
3. **Material credibility:** Paper grain, fine rules, label-like metadata, and data-ink accents make the product feel audited and human-made.
4. **Actionable contrast:** Moss denotes stable or positive movement, burnt vermilion marks anomalies or attention, and electric chartreuse is reserved for the active operational state.

### Color Philosophy
The base is an off-white **field paper** that leaves the interface breathable and legible. **Moss ink** conveys climate intelligence with restraint, while **night charcoal** anchors the operational shell. **Signal vermilion** is not decorative; it is reserved for anomalous activity, risk, and the “detect” stage. A thin **chartreuse signal** is used as a memorable action cue and should never dominate a full section.

### Layout Paradigm
Use a **sequence-led narrative** rather than a centered marketing stack. The desktop hero splits into a left editorial manifesto and a right live-looking operational dossier. The site continues as a horizontal process tape, alternating full-bleed narrative blocks with asymmetrical metric panels, then ends in a pilot-readiness ledger. On small screens, this collapses to an ordered field-report sequence.

### Signature Elements
1. A **vertical annotation rail** with phase labels, source notes, and simulated-data stamps.
2. A **process tape** showing MONITOR → DETECT → PREDICT → SIMULATE → RECOMMEND → ACT → MEASURE as an interrupted, active route.
3. **Cut-corner evidence cards** with small provenance lines such as “SIMULATED FIXTURE · TRACEABLE ENGINE.”

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like inspecting a field report: focused, tactile, and consequence-led. Clicking a mission phase changes the active story and data excerpt; a simulator control updates transparent projection outputs; source-boundary details are revealed on demand. Buttons communicate a real outcome or explain that a public page is a presentation surface; no deceptive controls.

### Animation
Motion is restrained and meaningful. The process tape advances with a 180ms transform/opacity transition. The hero dossier uses a single slow, low-contrast scanning line only when reduced motion is not requested. Evidence cards lift 2px on hover, status dots pulse softly only for active states, and entrance motion follows a 40ms stagger with a 220ms custom ease-out. All non-essential animation is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** supplies the thoughtful, civic-facing display voice for major editorial statements and key metrics. **Manrope** provides a clear operational sans for UI, tables, labels, and body text. Display headings are compact with tight tracking; labels are small, uppercase, widely tracked, and always paired with plain-language copy.

### Brand Essence
**EcoSphere AI is a transparent sustainability mission-control pilot for campuses that need to turn resource signals into traceable climate action.**

**Personality:** exacting, grounded, optimistic.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and evidence-aware. CTAs use action language tied to a demonstration or a decision rather than generic conversion language.

Example lines:
- “See the signal before it becomes a sustainability blind spot.”
- “Model the intervention. Keep the numbers traceable.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The symbol is a bold **orbital field marker**: three incomplete concentric arcs intersected by a measured vertical axis, suggesting a planet, a sensor field, and an active feedback loop. It is used without text at small scale; the wordmark combines the serif “EcoSphere” with a compact technical “AI.”

### Signature Brand Color
**Field Chartreuse — #C8F25B**

## Style Decisions

- Public messaging must state that the AIEM Campus dataset is simulated and that forecasts and intervention impacts are modeled estimates.
- Numerical claims must be framed as outputs from a deterministic calculation engine; AI provides explanation and action framing only.
- The website may explain the deployment roadmap but must not imply certified compliance, live Odoo connectivity, enterprise authentication, public deployment, or guaranteed savings.
